Business & Strategy: The People Who Make the Call

Someone decides which designs get made, what they cost, and which stores get them. That someone has one of the best-paying jobs in fashion.

⏱ 3 minute read
Salary Range
$90k–$120k+
Senior brand leaders at major companies can go well beyond this.
Typical Education
~4 years
Usually business, marketing, or fashion merchandising degrees.

What this path really is

Picture a brand as a ship. Designers build the beautiful sails, but somebody has to steer. That is this path. Business and strategy people answer the make-or-break questions: Which of these 50 designs do we actually produce? What price feels right but still makes money? Which celebrity should wear it first, and is the deal worth it?

The best part? You do not need to draw at all. You need to notice things. Why is everyone at school suddenly wearing that one brand? Why did that product sell out in an hour? If your brain automatically asks those questions, you are already doing this job for free.

Jobs behind this door

💎

Brand Manager

Owns a brand's whole story: how it looks, sounds, and feels everywhere it shows up. Protects the brand like it is a character.

🛒

Merchandiser

Decides what a store sells, how much, and at what price. Half fashion sense, half math, all strategy.

💰

Buyer

Shops for a living, seriously. Picks next season's products months ahead, betting the store's money on their taste.

Designers make things beautiful. Strategists make them unavoidable.
