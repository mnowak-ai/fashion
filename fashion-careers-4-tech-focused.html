What this path really is

Fashion used to guess what people would want. Now it measures. Every search, like, save, and sell-out leaves a data trail, and tech-focused fashion careers turn that trail into predictions. A trend forecaster studies what millions of people are watching and wearing, then tells brands what to make next year. Guess right, and a company earns millions. That is why they pay for it.

The other half of this path is digital design: building the online stores, virtual try-ons, and 3D clothing samples that let a brand test a design before sewing a single stitch. If you are the person who edits the videos, spots the trend early, or makes things look clean on a screen, this door was built for you. Bonus: it can be the fastest door to enter, sometimes just two years of school.
