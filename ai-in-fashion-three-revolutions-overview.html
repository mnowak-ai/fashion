As you read this series, watch for the same pattern in every story: AI does the heavy lifting, humans make the call. The AI drafts a hundred sketches; the designer picks one. The AI flags a rising trend; the forecaster decides if it is real. The AI suggests a price; the brand manager approves it. The tool is powerful. The person holding it is still the point.
