A real example, start to finish

Imagine a student named Maya who wants to launch a small line of festival jackets. On Monday she types her idea into the Design Studio and reacts to dozens of AI drafts until one feels right. On Tuesday she generates AI model photos of her jacket, no camera needed. On Wednesday she runs virtual try-ons to check how it looks across body types. On Thursday she exports a tech pack to send to a small manufacturer. On Friday she uses the branding tools to draft a logo and mood board for her launch post.

Five days, one app, zero photoshoots. That workflow used to require a team and a budget. Now it requires judgment, which, as you know by now, is the one thing the AI cannot supply.

The app supplies the speed. The designer supplies the "yes, that one."
