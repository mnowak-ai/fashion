⏱ 3 minute read

Words in, designs out

The biggest change AI brought to fashion design sounds almost too simple: you can now turn plain text into fashion sketches. Type "a puffy silver jacket inspired by astronauts" and an AI image tool hands you a page of ideas in seconds. None of them are finished designs. All of them are starting points, and starting points used to be the slowest part of the job.

It goes beyond sketches. Designers use AI to generate patterns, textures, and mood boards, the collages of colors and images that set the feeling for a whole collection. And when it is time to show off the work, AI helps edit photos and videos for portfolios and reels, cleaning backgrounds and polishing clips that once required expensive software skills.

So what is left for the human? The most important part: direction. AI produces options. It does not know which option is good. That judgment, called taste, is now the designer's superpower, and directing AI well is a skill you can start practicing today. In fact, practice right now.

The skill behind the tool

You just discovered the quiet truth of AI design: the quality of what comes out depends on the quality of what goes in. Vague words get vague sketches. Specific, vivid direction gets sketches worth building. In the industry this is becoming its own respected skill, and students who practice it now will walk into studios already fluent.

AI drafts fast. Humans decide what is worth making.
