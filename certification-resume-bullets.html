Four quick rules before you paste anything

Start with a verb. Designed, launched, analyzed, produced, directed. Verbs make you the one who acted, not the one who attended. Keep it to one line. Hiring managers skim; a bullet that wraps to a second line gets half-read. If yours runs long, cut words until it fits. Add a number when it is true. Fourteen drafts, three pieces, five sales: numbers turn claims into facts. Keep only what you can defend. If an interviewer says "tell me about this," you should light up, not freeze. Your capstone gave you that story. Use it.
