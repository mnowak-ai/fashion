Four Doors Into Fashion. One Has Your Name on It.

Fashion is not one job. It is hundreds of jobs sorted into four big paths. In the next two minutes, you will meet all four and find out which one fits you best.

⏱ 2 minute read

Here is the map

When people say "I could never work in fashion," they usually mean "I cannot sketch a dress." But drawing is only one door into this industry. There are three more, and most people have never been shown them.

Some fashion careers are about creating. Some are about deciding. Some are about data and screens. And some are about working with your hands, right there in the room where it happens. Different skills, different school paths, different paychecks. All of them are real fashion.

🎨

Design & Creative

The artists. They dream up the clothes, costumes, and fabrics the world wears next.

$80k–$160k+
💼

Business & Strategy

The deciders. They choose what gets made, what it costs, and how it wins.

$90k–$120k+
📱

Tech Focused

The predictors and builders. They use data and design tools to see the future first.

$70k–$80k+
🧺

Hands-On

The makers and fixers. They style, sew, and run the show behind the scenes.

$45k–$60k+

Before you pick, know this

Each path asks for something different. Some want a four-year degree. Some can start after a one or two-year program. Some pay more but take longer to enter. There is no "best" path, only the best path for how you like to work. So let's find yours.
