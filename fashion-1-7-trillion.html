$1.7 Trillion

That is how much the global fashion industry is worth.

It is one of the biggest industries on Earth, and it employs millions of people. Here is why that number should matter to you.

⏱ 3 minute read

A number too big to picture

Trillion is one of those words we hear and just skip past. So slow down for a second. A trillion is a million millions. If you spent one dollar every single second, day and night, it would take you more than 31,000 years to spend one trillion dollars.

Fashion is worth 1.7 of those. Every hoodie, every sneaker drop, every red carpet look, every ad you scroll past adds to it. And that money does not sit in a vault somewhere. It flows through millions of paychecks in almost every country in the world.

Bigger than Hollywood
The entire global movie industry brings in under $100 billion a year. Fashion is worth more than 17 movie industries stacked on top of each other.

Where do the millions of jobs come from?

Here is the mistake most people make. They hear "fashion jobs" and picture two things: famous designers and famous models. But those two jobs are a tiny slice of the industry. The rest of it is filled with jobs most people have never even thought about.

Somebody buys the cotton. Somebody runs the factory floor. Somebody prices the shoes, ships the boxes, designs the website, writes the ads, plans the store layout, studies what teenagers are wearing this month, and negotiates the deal that puts a celebrity in the front row. Every one of those is a paid career, and companies are hiring for all of them right now.

Fashion does not have one door in. It has thousands.
