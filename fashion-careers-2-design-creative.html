What this path really is

Designers do not just "draw pretty clothes." They solve problems with style. A costume designer has to make an actor look like a pirate and let them sword-fight without ripping a seam. A textile designer invents the actual fabric, deciding how it feels, stretches, and holds color. A fashion designer turns a feeling, like "confidence" or "summer," into something you can hang in a closet.

The secret skill here is not talent. It is taste plus stubbornness. Designers sketch fifty versions to find the one. They take feedback, rework, and try again. If you have ever redone a drawing or an outfit until it felt exactly right, you already know the feeling this job runs on.
