Step 4

Your Easy Path, Start to Finish

Here is the whole capstone as one simple walk. Follow the golden path, one step at a time. Nothing here is hard, it is just new.

1
Name your label above
Type it in Step 1. Short and bold. Do not overthink it, founders rebrand all the time.
2
Pick your three pieces
Choose three items in Step 2 that could share one story. A jacket, jeans, and sneakers feel like a family. A dress, shorts, and a sweater might not.
Tip: think "one outfit, three ways"
3
Build each prompt with the chips
In Step 3, tap through your three tabs and pick an era, color, material, and detail for each piece. Keep the same color story on all three so the collection matches.
4
Make each prompt yours
This is the most important step. Read your prompt out loud. Does it describe the piece in your head? If not, change the words until it does. Want "storm gray with silver zippers"? Say exactly that. Not sure how to word it? Paste the prompt into ChatGPT or Claude and ask: "Rewrite this prompt to match my vision: [describe your idea]."
Your added words = your signature
5
Open your design studio
Go to thenewblack.ai, create a free account, and open the Design Studio.
6
Paste, generate, react
Paste your first prompt and generate. Look at the result like a boss reviewing work: What is right? What is off? Adjust a word or two and generate again. Two or three rounds usually finds the winner.
Never settle for draft one
7
Repeat for pieces two and three
Same process, same color story. Watch your collection start to look like it belongs on one rack.
8
Save your designs + copy your brief
Save or screenshot your three favorite designs. Then scroll down, copy your finished Collection Brief, and submit both together. That is the capstone, complete.
Designs + brief = done 🏆
